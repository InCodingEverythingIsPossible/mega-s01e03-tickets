The fix queued for #4471 fixes the parser. It does not fix the booking-v3 hint: those reports stay open after it ships.

The problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant books 2 December and the invite says 12 February. Every channel, every tenant on file.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour run booking-v3 in day-first locales. Their people type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The result looks like the parser bug, but only through the form, and the parser fix leaves it in place. The fix is the hint, or reading the field the way the hint says.

Read as a one-day move

Some parser reports describe a date that moves by one day, not a swap: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” “The invite arrived before I made the booking.” The desk read them that way (desk-notes.json) and counts them where they are filed, under parser. A swap never moves a date by one day. Re-read them after the parser fix ships: if they stay, they are a time-zone bug and need a ticket of their own.

Why intake got the split wrong

Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser: an en-US browser is tagged hint. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and form (causes.ts), so intake’s tags and the table above disagree. Count by tenant, not by browser.

Lessons

  • A merge on subject line counts symptoms. Different bugs share a symptom, so the count was right and the “one issue” was not.
  • Before a big ticket goes to known, read the rows that don’t fit the pattern.
